Security & Implementation FAQs

Common questions about our methodology and engagement process

What security standards do your VAPT services follow?

Our assessments align with OWASP testing guidelines, PTES methodology, and industry standards including ISO 27001 controls. We provide findings mapped to CVSS scoring for prioritized remediation.

What is the typical timeline for a VAPT engagement?

Most engagements complete within 5-10 business days depending on scope. Critical findings are communicated within 24 hours. We provide executive and technical reports with remediation guidance.

Do you offer ongoing security monitoring after assessment?

Yes, we offer retesting packages and continuous monitoring options. Many clients engage us for quarterly security reviews and annual compliance assessments.
